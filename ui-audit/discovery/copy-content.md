## Summary
Interface copy in Altair OS is bimodal: the redesigned MC v2 surfaces have genuinely strong copy (helpful next-action empty states, a thoughtful error-mapping layer in shared/lib/operational-errors.ts, per-domain status label maps, centralized company-timezone date formatting), while the older generation of surfaces — reports chart cards, analytics/dispatch stat strips, the technician app, and critically the two public customer-facing billing pages — carries a different voice (Title Case, "Failed to X." errors, "Quote" instead of "Estimate", OS-native confirm dialogs). The single most damaging finding is not tonal: the one shared formatCurrency rounds to whole dollars and is used on invoice line items, totals, billing emails, and the public Pay Now amount, so customers see "$1,235" and are charged $1,234.56 by Stripe. Terminology drift is real but tractable — five clusters (Trash/Recently Deleted, Approved/Accepted, Overdue/Past due, Team/Users/Members/Technicians, Work/Jobs) each have a clear canonical winner already dominant in the codebase. Quantified splits: 633 sentence-case vs 138 Title Case labels, 311 "We couldn't" vs 194 "Failed to" errors, 234 "cancelled" vs 14 "canceled", and every in-app browser tab titled just "Altair OS". Most fixes are string sweeps with existing conventions as the target, not design work.

## Findings

### [P1/SYSTEMIC] All billing amounts round to whole dollars — including the public 'Pay Now' amount the customer is about to be charged in exact cents
- category: currency-trust | effort: MEDIUM
- evidence: shared/types/customer.ts:171-178 — the single shared formatCurrency uses Intl.NumberFormat with maximumFractionDigits: 0 (rounds $1,234.56 → "$1,235"). 380 call sites across 85 files import it, including every customer-facing billing surface: shared/components/invoices/PublicInvoicePayNowCard.tsx:51 (the amount above the Pay Now button on app/invoice-payment/[token]/page.tsx), shared/components/billing/BillingLineItemsList.tsx (9 uses — unit prices like $89.50 render as "$90"), shared/components/billing/BillingTotalsSummary.tsx (13 uses — subtotal/tax/total/balance due), shared/components/billing/InvoiceAmountDueHero.tsx (7), and lib/email/billing-email-layout.ts (20 uses — invoice/estimate emails). Contrast: shared/types/job-material.ts:41 uses maximumFractionDigits: 2 and lib/database/services/operational-notifications.ts:15-23 uses the Intl default (2 digits), so cents appear in some notifications but not on the invoice itself.
- why: Stripe charges the exact balanceDue in cents, so the customer clicks Pay Now on "$1,235" and their card statement shows $1,234.56 — a direct trust break on a payment flow. On invoice/estimate documents and emails, rounded line items and rounded tax visibly fail to sum to the rounded total ($49.50 + $49.50 can display as $50 + $50 = $99). Whole-dollar rounding is fine for dashboard KPIs but is being applied to quasi-legal billing documents.
- suggestion: Add a cents-always formatCurrencyExact (minimumFractionDigits: 2) and require it on every billing document, payment surface, and billing email; keep the whole-dollar variant for glance KPIs only. The fix is one new function plus a targeted sweep of shared/components/billing/*, shared/components/invoices/Public*, and lib/email/billing-email-layout.ts.

### [P2/SYSTEMIC] Two coexisting error voices: robotic "Failed to X." (194 strings) vs the house "We couldn't X — what to do next" (311 strings)
- category: error-voice | effort: MEDIUM
- evidence: grep counts: 194 user-facing "Failed to …" strings vs 311 "We couldn't … / Could not …" strings across shared/ + app/ + lib/ (active surfaces). Both voices ship on the same screens: shared/components/dispatch/DispatchPageView.tsx:175 "Failed to assign job." vs shared/components/jobs/JobsPageView.tsx:647 "We couldn't update the selected jobs."; shared/components/settings/TeamMembersTable.tsx:159,293-294 "Failed to update member role." / "Failed to suspend team member." The mapping layer shared/lib/operational-errors.ts:25-43 (formatActionError) shows the intended voice (recovery guidance, "Refresh the page and choose again") but line 42 passes any unmapped server string straight through, so whichever voice the action author used reaches the user verbatim.
- why: The "We couldn't… try again / contact your office" voice (with data-preservation reassurance like OFFLINE_ACTION_ERROR at operational-errors.ts:131) is genuinely good; the terse "Failed to X." strings read as developer logging, offer no next step, and make failure feel like the user's fault. The 50/50 split makes error quality feel random screen to screen.
- suggestion: Adopt one error template (couldn't + object + next step), sweep the 194 "Failed to" strings (they are short and mechanical to rewrite), and have formatActionError apply the template as its final fallback instead of returning the raw string.

## Proposed voice principles

1. **Sentence case, everywhere.** Buttons, headers, pills, chart titles, stat labels. Capitals only for proper nouns, record IDs (INV-1042), and product names. (633 strings already comply; sweep the 138.)
2. **Money shows cents wherever money changes hands.** Any amount a customer can pay, any line item, total, or ledger row gets exact cents; whole-dollar rounding only for glance KPIs — never both on one surface.
3. **One name per concept, chosen once.** Estimate (never Quote), Approved (never Accepted), Overdue (never past-due for tenant invoices), Recently Deleted (never Trash), team member (never user). New synonyms require editing the vocabulary doc, not just the screen.
4. **Errors say what we couldn't do, then what to do next.** Template: "We couldn't [verb + object]. [What survived]. [Next step]." Never "Failed to X." — and formatActionError enforces the template as its fallback.
5. **Empty states sell the next action, not the absence.** "Add your first customer — customers unlock jobs, estimates, and service history" over "No data". (Existing MC v2 empty states are the reference standard.)
6. **Destructive confirmations name the object, the consequence, and the reversibility window** ("Move Sarah Nguyen to Recently Deleted? Hidden for 60 days.") — always in AltairConfirmDialog, never window.confirm, with the confirm button restating the verb.
7. **Dates, times, and enums never render raw.** All dates through shared/lib/datetime.ts in the company timezone; all enum values through a shared humanizeEnum or a domain label map.
8. **Typography is part of voice.** "…" not "...", American spelling (canceled), "—" for empty values, tabular numerals for counts.

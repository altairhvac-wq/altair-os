- Company time zone (migration `220`) — `companies.timezone_set_at` is the one record of whether a company has chosen its time zone; the zone's name is never read as a signal. `resolveCompanyTimeZone` (`shared/lib/datetime.ts`) takes the row and is called once where a row is read (the company context; `lib/database/queries/company-timezone.ts` for reminders, the overdue sweep, estimate approval routing and the draft invoice made on completion); everything downstream uses the resolved zone as it is. Chosen: the stored zone exactly, Eastern included (before this, a company that picked Eastern ran on Mountain everywhere). Never chosen: what the company always ran on (the `America/New_York` column default resolves to Mountain, any other stored zone is used as stored), and an owner or admin sees a "Confirm your time zone" card on the dashboard ("Not now" puts it away for that browser) and on Settings → Company, pre-selected from the browser's zone (`TimeZoneConfirmPrompt`, `updateCompanyTimezoneAction`). The migration marks as chosen only rows holding a zone other than `America/New_York` or `America/Denver`; it moves nobody's times. A choice is recorded by the confirm card, by step 1 of the guided setup (which asks for the time zone and opens on the browser's zone for a company that has not chosen, saying so and, when that differs from the zone the company runs on, that saving moves its times; a suggestion left untouched never replaces a zone confirmed in the meantime), by changing the zone in the company profile form (an unchanged zone on that form is not a choice), and at sign-up: the sign-up and company setup forms send the browser's zone, kept on the user as `time_zone` until the company exists and written once to a company made in the last ten minutes that has no choice yet (`recordDetectedTimeZoneForNewCompany`). A browser that reports only UTC is treated as "zone not known" everywhere (`isPlaceTimeZone`): nothing is recorded or suggested from it. The draft invoice made when a job is completed now dates itself in the resolved zone like everything else (for a company that has never chosen it used the stored Eastern default). The same migration gives `get_company_report_daily_series` a fourth argument, `p_time_zone` (default `UTC`): the dashboard's performance chart and monthly target card now count a job in the month the company finished it, not the UTC month; the Reports page still calls it with three arguments and counts UTC days, as its headline figures (migration `169`) do. Before migration `220` has run everything behaves as it did: reads fall back when the column is missing, the step 1 list and the confirm card are not shown, and the dashboard's four-argument call falls back to the three-argument one.

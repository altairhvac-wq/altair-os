# Altair Visual Prompt Library

**Status:** Working Draft  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Purpose:** Create consistent AI-generated images and videos that follow the Altair Canon.

---

## 1. Governing Document

Every prompt in this library must follow:

`docs/foundation/ALTAIR_CANON.md`

The Canon (including the Lifestyle Bible sections) defines the Altair creative world.

This Prompt Library translates that world into reusable instructions for AI image and video generators.

If a generated image conflicts with the Canon, the image should be rejected even when it is visually attractive.

3. Master Negative Direction

Add this direction to every prompt when the image generator supports negative prompts.

Avoid neon, RGB lighting, blue LED lighting, harsh commercial lighting, gaudy gold, excessive chrome, glossy plastic, mirrored furniture, fake marble, oversized logos, influencer aesthetics, excessive displays of wealth, clutter, visual chaos, extreme HDR, oversaturation, fisheye distortion, impossible architecture, distorted furniture, malformed objects, artificial-looking people, excessive background blur, visible text, captions, watermarks, and brand names.
4. Altair Consistency Rules

Every generated image should follow these rules:

The lifestyle must feel earned rather than displayed.
Luxury must come from materials, space, craftsmanship, and restraint.
Spaces must feel realistic, functional, and livable.
Technology should be integrated quietly.
Lighting should be warm, layered, and architectural.
Rooms should feel minimal but not empty.
Objects should appear useful and intentionally selected.
People should appear natural rather than posed.
Visible branding should be minimal.
Every image should feel like part of the same world.
5. Default Camera Rules

Use the following camera choices consistently.

Expansive architecture
28mm architectural lens, eye-level perspective, straight vertical lines, realistic room proportions
Default environmental scene
35mm environmental lens, natural eye-level perspective, balanced architectural composition
Lifestyle moment
50mm lens, natural perspective, subtle depth of field, subject shown within the surrounding environment
Portrait or object detail
85mm lens, restrained depth of field, realistic detail, soft natural background separation

Do not use extreme wide-angle or fisheye perspectives.

6. Default Lighting Options

Select one lighting direction for each image.

Early morning
Soft sunrise light entering through large windows, warm interior lights still glowing, calm shadows, quiet beginning-of-day atmosphere
Daylight
Soft natural daylight, controlled highlights, warm neutral interior lighting, realistic shadows
Golden hour
Warm golden-hour sunlight, long soft shadows, natural reflections, calm cinematic atmosphere
Evening
Warm concealed 2700K architectural lighting, subtle firelight, dark exterior visible through glass, calm intimate atmosphere
Night
Layered warm architectural lighting, illuminated stone and wood textures, restrained contrast, no blue or RGB lighting

8. Human Presence Rules

When people appear:

They should look natural and occupied with a real activity.
They should not stare directly at the camera unless the image is intentionally a portrait.
Clothing should be practical, refined, and understated.
Avoid visible luxury-brand logos.
Avoid exaggerated model-like poses.
Families should feel candid and believable.
Employees should be portrayed as capable professionals.
Customers should be portrayed respectfully.
Diversity should appear naturally across the complete image library.
Hands, tools, vehicles, and work activities must appear physically realistic.
9. Recurring Environment

To improve consistency, the initial image collection should feel like it follows one connected service-business owner through the same world.

The recurring world may include:

One established residential service company
One primary office and operations facility
One primary modern-organic residence
One recurring service vehicle design
One consistent architectural material palette
One recurring family
One recurring leadership team
Several connected travel destinations

The imagery does not need to show the same person's face clearly in every image.

Consistency should come primarily from the architecture, materials, lighting, camera language, emotional tone, and story progression.
